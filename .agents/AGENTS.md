## 2. Candlestick Anatomy & Single Candle Rules (Pages 5–10)
*   **Rejection Shadows**: For Hammer, Inverted Hammer, Shooting Star, and Hanging Man patterns, the rejection shadow (wick) must be **at least $2\times$ the size of the real body**.
    *   *Hammer*: Body at top, long lower wick $\ge 2\times$ body. Forms in a downtrend (Bullish Reversal).
    *   *Hanging Man*: Same shape as Hammer, forms in an uptrend (Bearish Reversal).
    *   *Shooting Star*: Body at bottom, long upper wick $\ge 2\times$ body. Forms in an uptrend (Bearish Reversal).
    *   *Inverted Hammer*: Same shape as Shooting Star, forms in a downtrend (Bullish Reversal).
*   **Doji Indecision** (Body size $\le 6\%$ of total range):
    *   *Dragonfly Doji*: Flat top (upper wick $\le 5\%$ of range), long lower shadow. Indicates bullish rejection of lower prices.
    *   *Gravestone Doji*: Flat bottom (lower wick $\le 5\%$ of range), long upper shadow. Indicates bearish rejection of higher prices.
    *   *Long-Legged Doji*: Long wicks on both sides ($\ge 25\%$ of range each). Indicates extreme volatility and indecision.
    *   *Standard Doji*: Tiny body with wicks on both sides, closing near equilibrium.
*   **Spinning Tops** (Body size between $6\%$ and $25\%$ of range): Small body in the middle with equal or near-equal shadows (difference $\le 20\%$ of range). Wait for breakout of Spinning Top's range.
    *   *Bullish Spinning Top*: Spinning Top with green body.
    *   *Bearish Spinning Top*: Spinning Top with red body.
*   **Marubozu** (Body size $\ge 90\%$ of total range): Long solid body with little or no wicks.
    *   *Bullish Marubozu*: Green body; shows aggressive buying momentum.
    *   *Bearish Marubozu*: Red body; shows aggressive selling momentum.

## 3. Dual & Triple Candle Rules (Pages 11–15)
*   **Bullish Engulfing**: A green candle body completely engulfs the preceding red candle body.
*   **Bearish Engulfing**: A red candle body completely engulfs the preceding green candle body.
*   **Tweezer Tops/Bottoms**: Two consecutive candles with equal or near-equal highs (Tweezer Top) or lows (Tweezer Bottom) within $0.05\%$. Signals double rejection.
*   **Dark Cloud Cover**: A green candle followed by a red candle opening above its high and closing below its 50% body midpoint.
*   **Piercing Line**: A red candle followed by a green candle opening below its low and closing above its 50% body midpoint.
*   **Morning Star (Triple Candle)**: Red candle ➔ Small body gap-down candle ➔ Big green candle closing past 50% of the red candle body (Bullish Reversal).
*   **Evening Star (Triple Candle)**: Green candle ➔ Small body gap-up candle ➔ Big red candle closing past 50% of the green candle body (Bearish Reversal).
*   **Three White Soldiers**: Three consecutive strong green candles opening within the previous body and closing near the high.
*   **Three Black Crows**: Three consecutive strong red candles opening within the previous body and closing near the low.

## 4. Reversal Chart Pattern Rules (Pages 16–25)
*   **Double Top (M-Pattern)**: Two peaks at the same resistance. Enter short only on a breakout close below the Neckline. Stop Loss goes above the second peak.
*   **Double Bottom (W-Pattern)**: Two troughs at the same support. Enter long only on a breakout close above the Neckline. Stop Loss goes below the second trough.
*   **Head and Shoulders (Bearish Reversal)**: Left Shoulder, higher Head, and Right Shoulder. Enter short on a close below the Neckline. Stop Loss goes above the Right Shoulder. Target is the head-to-neckline height projected downwards.
*   **Inverted Head and Shoulders (Bullish Reversal)**: Left Shoulder, deeper Head, and Right Shoulder. Enter long on a close above the Neckline. Stop Loss goes below the Right Shoulder.
*   **Cup and Handle (Bullish Continuation/Reversal)**:
    *   *Rounded Cup*: The cup must be a gradual, rounded curve representing accumulation, never a sharp V-shape.
    *   *Handle Retracement Constraint*: **The handle must not retrace by more than 33% (one-third) of the cup's total depth.** If it retraces deeper, the pattern is invalidated.
    *   *Entry*: Buy on a breakout above the handle's resistance line.

## 5. Continuation Pattern Rules (Pages 26–29)
*   **Ascending Triangle**: Flat horizontal resistance and ascending diagonal support (higher lows). Shows buyers are aggressive. Enter long on a close above flat resistance.
*   **Descending Triangle**: Flat horizontal support and descending diagonal resistance (lower highs). Shows sellers are aggressive. Enter short on a close below flat support.
*   **Symmetrical Triangle**: Converging upper and lower trendlines. Volume must contract during the formation. Enter in the direction of the breakout.

## 6. Trendlines & Channels (Pages 30–31)
*   **Trendline Validation**: A trendline requires **at least 2 swing points to draw** and a **3rd touch point to validate**. Do not force trendlines; let them connect naturally.
*   **Channels**: Tighter parallel trendlines. In an uptrend channel, buy at support (lower boundary) and take profit at resistance (upper boundary). In a downtrend channel, short at resistance (upper boundary) and take profit at support (lower boundary).

## 7. Volume Conviction & Climax Rules (Pages 33–36)
*   **Volume Matrix**:
    *   *Price UP + Volume UP* = Valid bullish extension (Buy pullbacks).
    *   *Price DOWN + Volume UP* = Valid bearish extension (Sell rallies).
    *   *Price UP + Volume DOWN* = Buying exhaustion (Do not chase; look to fade).
    *   *Price DOWN + Volume DOWN* = Selling exhaustion (Do not chase; look to fade).
*   **Volume Climax Reversals**:
    *   An extreme volume spike (**$3\times$ to $5\times$ of the 20-period average volume**) printing at the end of a trend represents a volume climax (exhaustion).
    *   *Action*: Look to enter a counter-trend fade position once a confirming rejection candle closes.

## 8. Smart Money Liquidity Sweeps (Pages 37–38)
*   **Liquidity Pools**:
    *   *Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL)*: Resting buy-stop orders sitting above major swing highs.
    *   *Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL)*: Resting sell-stop orders sitting below major swing lows.
*   **The Sweep Setup**:
    *   *Long Entry (Buy CE)*: Price sweeps below a major swing low or Yesterday's Low, triggers the SSL, but immediately bounces to close back above the level. Enter long on the close of the sweep candle; Stop Loss goes below the sweep low.
    *   *Short Entry (Buy PE)*: Price sweeps above a major swing high or Yesterday's High, triggers the BSL, but immediately rejects to close back above the level. Enter short on the close of the sweep candle; Stop Loss goes above the sweep high.

## 9. Technical & Environment Constraints
*   **Windows ESM Absolute Imports**: Always prefix absolute paths in ES modules (`.mjs`) with the `file:///` protocol (e.g., `import('file:///C:/...')`) to prevent Node.js resolution failures on Windows.
*   **Weekend Scanner Bypass**: Ensure that daily intraday scanners (Doji and Proximity) explicitly check for weekdays (`Mon-Fri`) on startup, manual endpoints, and intervals to prevent executing database scans when the market is closed.
*   **Daily Close Alignment**: Always calculate daily pivot levels for sub-daily charts using the official Daily (`D`) candle Close price (the VWAP close) instead of using the last 5m candle Close price (LTP).

## 10. Options Premium Level Touch & OTM Selling Rules
*   **The Lunchtime S4 Discount Trap (Buying Setup)**:
    *   *Action*: Place buy limit orders at the S4 level of Call options during the lunchtime consolidation (11:30 AM – 1:30 PM IST), stop loss below S5, and target the daily midpoint or R3 during the afternoon K-L-M periods (after 2:15 PM) breakout push.
*   **The R5 Extreme Short (Option Selling/Writing)**:
    *   *Action*: Short OTM Call/Put premiums when they touch their own R5 resistance, with a strict 50% premium stop loss (Entry $\times 1.5$), and cover the position at ₹1.0 or let expire worthless at ₹0.
*   **The Index Direction Filter**:
    *   *Action*: Never write/short Call options if the main index is in a strong uptrend (above daily Open), and never write/short Put options if the main index is under severe bearish pressure (below daily Open or first-hour PCR velocity < -0.03).
*   **The Decay Proximity Sweet Spot**:
    *   *Action*: OTM short positions entered 2-3 days before weekly expiry (Friday and Monday sessions) have an exceptionally high win rate on R3/R4 touches, as the accelerated time-decay curve (theta) rapidly deflates premium spikes.
*   **The S3/S4 Candlestick Confluence (Option Buying Setup)**:
    *   *Bullish Marubozu Confirmation*: If the option premium touches S3/S4 and closes as a solid green **Bullish Marubozu** (body size $\ge 85\%$ of total range), buy immediately with a stop loss below S5 (Win Rate: 62.5%, net +1242 pts).
    *   *Bearish Marubozu Invalidation*: If the option premium touches S3/S4 but closes as a solid red **Bearish Marubozu** (body size $\ge 85\%$ of total range), **do not buy**. This indicates a support slip with a 78.6% failure rate.
    *   *Doji/Standard Invalidation*: Avoid buying raw touches that form standard or Doji candles, as they indicate lacking conviction (Win Rate: ~39%). Always wait for solid candle rejection.
*   **Stock Options Specific Rules (Monthly Expiries)**:
    *   *Expiry Calendar Logic*: NSE monthly stock options expire on the **last Tuesday of the month** (use `YYMMDD` format based on last Tuesday date, e.g. `260728`).
    *   *Stock Buying Confluence*: On the 30m chart, wait for S3/S4 touch candles to close. Enter long *only* on **Bullish Marubozu** (70.6% win rate) or **Doji** (68.8% win rate) closes. Avoid standard and red candle touches.
    *   *Stock Selling R5 Constraint*: Writing stock options at R5 has a lower win rate (53.5%) due to monthly contract swings. Avoid writing stock options at R5 *unless* trading in the final week before expiry, or use a wider 75% - 100% premium stop-loss.
