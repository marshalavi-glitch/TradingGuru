2. KEY STRUCTURAL PATTERNS AND TRADING RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. OPEN INSIDE VALUE (Between S2 and R2)
   - Frequency: Occurred 206 times (37% of all days).
   - Win Rate : Closed green 48.1% of the time (perfectly balanced 50/50).
   - Behavior : This is the most common day type. The market consolidates inside
                yesterday's range. Average open-to-close trend is flat (+4.3 pts).
   - Rule     : Avoid chasing breakouts before 11:30 AM. Sell call options at
                VAH resistance (R3/R4) and sell put options at VAL support (S3/S4).

B. EXTREME GAP UPS (Open Above R6)
   - Frequency: Occurred 25 times in 2 years.
   - Win Rate : Closed RED 76.0% of the time!
   - Average  : -47.5 Nifty points move from Open to Close.
   - Behavior : This is a classic "Exhaustion Gap". Morning gap-chasers are trapped
                and institutions immediately book profit on their long swing positions.
   - Rule     : Fade this gap immediately. Buy Put Options (PE) on a 5-minute
                bearish candle rejection at the open, targeting Yesterday's VAH.

C. THE S4 STANDARD GAP DOWN DRIVE (Open Between S3 and S4)
   - Frequency: Occurred 61 times.
   - Win Rate : Closed RED 59.0% of the time.
   - Average  : -50.2 Nifty points move from Open to Close.
   - Behavior : Indicates "Gap Acceptance" by institutional sellers. They sell the
                opening bounce, creating a sustained bearish drive.
   - Rule     : This is a trending day. Do NOT buy support touches. Look to buy
                Put Options (PE) on pullbacks to the S3 level to ride the move down.

D. THE S6 PANIC CLIMAX REVERSAL (Open Below S6)
   - Frequency: Occurred 24 times.
   - Win Rate : Closed GREEN 62.5% of the time.
   - Average  : +24.3 Nifty points move from Open to Close.
   - Behavior : Panic selling reaches a climax, creating extreme value. Shorts quickly
                cover their positions and institutions step in to buy the discount.
   - Rule     : Buy Call Options (CE) on the first 5-minute green reversal candle
                close, placing the stop loss strictly below the daily open.
