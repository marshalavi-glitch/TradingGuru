3. KEY STRUCTURAL PATTERNS AND TRADING RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. VALUE AREA MIDPOINT OPENS (Between S2 and R2)
   - Frequency: Occurred 206 times in Nifty & 229 times in Bank Nifty.
   - Behavior : This is the equilibrium zone. The average open-to-close move is 
                very small (+4.3 pts Nifty, +42.3 pts Bank Nifty).
   - Rule     : Do not chase morning breakouts. Play reversion strategies. Sell
                Call Options at VAH (R3/R4) and Sell Put Options at VAL (S3/S4).

B. NIFTY EXTREME GAP UPS (Open Above R6)
   - Behavior : Closed RED 76% of the time, with an average decline of -47.5 pts
                from the open. This represents a classic Exhaustion Gap.
   - Rule     : Fade this gap immediately. Look for Put Option (PE) entries in the
                first 15 minutes of trading.

C. BANK NIFTY VALUE AREA TOP RESISTANCE (Open Between R2 and R3)
   - Behavior : Closed RED 60.4% of the time, with an average drop of -128.6 pts.
   - Rule     : When Bank Nifty opens just below R3, it struggles to accept higher
                prices. Look to buy Put Options (PE) on bearish wicks at R3.

D. THE PANIC CLIMAX REVERSALS (Open Below S6)
   - Behavior : Closed GREEN 62.5% of the time in Nifty (+24.3 pts average move)
                and 57.1% of the time in Bank Nifty (+110.8 pts average move).
   - Rule     : Extremely high risk-reward. Do not sell gaps down. Look for the
                first 5-minute green candle close to enter Call Options (CE) and
                ride the short-covering squeeze higher.
