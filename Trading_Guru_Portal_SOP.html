1. Executive Summary & Core Principles

The Trading Guru Portal is an institutional-grade, multi-asset algorithmic trading and market profile workstation designed for high-conviction decision making across Indian equities (NSE), index derivatives (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY), and commodities (MCX Crude Oil, Gold, Silver, Natural Gas).

Unlike retail platforms that rely on lagging technical indicators (e.g., RSI, MACD, Stochastics), the Trading Guru portal operates on **Raw Market Microstructure, Order Flow, Auction Market Theory (AMT), and Camarilla Equation Volatility Anchors**.

🏛️ The Four-Step Execution Framework

Every discretionary and algorithmic execution must rigorously follow the sequence:

1. DATA ➔ 2. STRUCTURE ➔ 3. CONTEXT ➔ 4. EXECUTION

  • Data: Real-time WebSocket ticks, volume footprints, depth orderbook imbalances.
  • Structure: Static Camarilla pivot boundaries, TPO value areas (VAH/VAL/POC), Initial Balance (IB).
  • Context: Relative market position, institutional sentiment (FII net long/short), first-hour PCR velocity.
  • Execution: Pre-defined entry trigger, mandatory structural stop-loss, dual-target scaling.

Core Tenets

  • Market Logic, Not Emotions: Execution must be triggered solely by confirmed structural breaches, sweep-reclaims, or institutional absorption. Subjective opinions, bias, or news sentiment are discarded.
  • Clean Chart Discipline: Only Price Action, Order Flow Bins, and Dynamic Volume Profiles are plotted. Redundant indicators that introduce visual clutter and execution latency are prohibited.
  • Strict Capital Preservation: Every trade maintains a hard mathematical risk ceiling. Chasing extended momentum past breakdown or breakout lines is systemically blocked.

4. Candlestick Anatomy & Raw Price Action Manual

Candlesticks represent real-time auction battles between aggressive buyers and aggressive sellers. Trading Guru enforces strict mathematical ratios to validate candle formations.

4.1 Rejection Shadow Rules (2x Wick Rule)

For high-conviction reversals (Hammer, Inverted Hammer, Shooting Star, Hanging Man), the rejection wick must be at least $2 imes$ the size of the real body.

Candle Pattern Structural Location Mathematical Criteria Trade Bias
Hammer End of Downtrend (Support / S3) Body at top; Lower Wick $ge 2 imes$ Body; Upper Wick $le 10%$ total range. Bullish Reversal
Shooting Star End of Uptrend (Resistance / R3) Body at bottom; Upper Wick $ge 2 imes$ Body; Lower Wick $le 10%$ total range. Bearish Reversal
Hanging Man Top of Uptrend (Resistance / R4) Same geometry as Hammer, but prints at peak of extended bullish move. Bearish Exhaustion
Inverted Hammer Bottom of Downtrend (Support / S4) Same geometry as Shooting Star, but prints at trough of bearish extension. Bullish Attempt

4.2 Doji Indecision & Balance Rules (Body $le 6%$ of Range)

  • Dragonfly Doji: Flat top (upper shadow $le 5%$ of total range) with long lower rejection tail. Indicates strong buyer absorption at lower prices.
  • Gravestone Doji: Flat bottom (lower shadow $le 5%$ of total range) with long upper rejection shadow. Indicates aggressive seller rejection at highs.
  • Long-Legged Doji: Long wicks extending symmetrically on both sides ($ge 25%$ range each). Signals extreme equilibrium and impending volatility expansion.
  • Standard Doji: Microscopic body closing at exact midpoint. Indicates temporary exhaustion; wait for range breakout.

4.3 Marubozu Momentum Rules (Body $ge 90%$ of Range)

A Marubozu represents absolute dominance by one market participant with near-zero shadows:

  • Bullish Marubozu (Green): Open = Low, Close = High. Confirms aggressive market buyers clearing all ask liquidity. High-probability continuation signal.
  • Bearish Marubozu (Red): Open = High, Close = Low. Confirms aggressive market sellers clearing all bid liquidity. High-probability breakdown signal.
⚠️ Options Level S3/S4 Confluence Rule

When an option premium touches S3 or S4, enter long ONLY if the touch candle closes as a Bullish Marubozu ($ge 85%$ body size; 62.5% historical win rate). If the candle closes as a Bearish Marubozu, do NOT enter (indicates support collapse with a 78.6% failure rate).

📌 Daily Pivot Alignment Rule

To eliminate sub-daily level distortion, Trading Guru strictly computes intraday pivots using the official Daily ($D$) Close price (the official Exchange VWAP close) rather than the last 5-minute closing tick (LTP).

6. Order Flow Footprint Engine

The Footprint Workstation dissects each candlestick into discrete price buckets, revealing actual contracts traded at the bid and ask.

6.1 Bid / Ask Mechanics

  • Left Side (Red / Bid Volume): Aggressive market sell orders matched against passive limit buy orders.
  • Right Side (Green / Ask Volume): Aggressive market buy orders matched against passive limit sell orders.
  • Delta: Ask Volume minus Bid Volume. Positive delta indicates net aggressive buying; negative delta indicates net aggressive selling.

6.2 Volume Imbalance Detection

An imbalance occurs when volume at a specific price level significantly outweighs the diagonally opposite price level. Trading Guru provides selectable multipliers: 1.5x, 2.0x, 3.0x, 4.0x.

  • Stacked Buying Imbalance: 3 or more consecutive green imbalance rows. Confirms aggressive institutional initiative buying. Serves as dynamic support on pullbacks.
  • Stacked Selling Imbalance: 3 or more consecutive red imbalance rows. Confirms aggressive institutional initiative selling. Serves as dynamic resistance on rallies.

6.3 Order Flow Signatures

Signature Visual Identifier Microstructure Context Trading Action
Absorption Huge volume bin with near-zero delta; price fails to advance. Passive institutional limit orders soaking up aggressive retail flow. Look to enter counter to the aggressive flow on confirmation.
Exhaustion Volume drops off dramatically at new extreme (e.g. 0 or single-digit contracts). Lack of aggressive market orders willing to trade at higher/lower prices. Fade the extreme; expect rapid mean reversion back to POC.
Unfinished Auction Volume trades at both bid and ask on the exact candle high or low. Price discovery incomplete; business remains uncompleted. Price will revisit and breach the unfinished extreme in future periods.
Delta Divergence Price prints higher high while session delta prints lower low (or vice versa). Effort vs Result divergence; momentum failing to support price movement. High-probability reversal setup in direction of delta.

7. Market Profile & Pro TPO Workstation

Market Profile organizes trading activity across 30-minute lettered brackets ($A$ through $M$) to display time spent at price, revealing Value Area (VA) and Point of Control (POC).

7.1 Profile Components

  • Initial Balance (IB): Range defined by periods $A$ and $B$ (09:15 to 10:15 IST). Sets the daily volatility baseline.
  • Point of Control (POC): The exact price level where the greatest number of TPO letters accumulated. Represents the fairest price to conduct business.
  • Value Area (70%): Price range containing 70% of total TPOs, bounded by **VAH (Value Area High)** and **VAL (Value Area Low)**.
  • Single Prints: Columns with only one letter. Represents rapid price discovery where price moved too quickly for balance to form. Acts as future support/resistance.
  • Buying / Selling Tails: Extreme single prints formed in periods $A$ or $M$ ($ge 3$ TPOs long). Signals aggressive long-term participant intervention.

7.2 Classical Day Types & Trading Playbook

Profile Shape Day Type Auction Meaning Execution Strategy

Normal Variation Extended Bell Moderate IB with range extension in $C$ or $D$ period ($le 2 imes$ IB). Trade direction of extension; pullback to IB high/low.
Trend Day Elongated Vertical Narrow IB; constant range extension every period. Multiple single prints. Go with the trend; never fade. Keep trailing stops below prior period lows.

9. Algo Trading Workstation: Rules & Execution

The Algo Trade Workstation automates high-probability price action setups with strict capital allocation, structural stop losses, and anti-churn safety filters.

9.1 Capital Allocation & Sizing Parameters

  • Total Capital Pool: ₹10,00,000 (10 Lakhs).
  • Maximum Concurrent Positions: 4 slots (₹2,50,000 allocated per position).
  • Position Sizing Equation: $ ext{Quantity} = lfloor ext{Max Capital Per Trade} / ext{Entry Price} floor$.
  • Anti-Churn Cooldown: 20 minutes mandatory lock on any symbol following an exit, preventing over-trading during chop.
  • Session Intraday Square-off: Auto square-off at 15:15 IST for equities; 23:15 IST for MCX commodities.

9.2 The Three Institutional Algo Strategies

STRATEGY 1: BUY REVERSAL (S3 RECLAIM) — LONG SETUP WIN RATE: ~65%

Core Concept: Smart Money Liquidity Sweep below major support followed by rapid reclaim.

  • Step 1 (Test): Price approaches S3 Buy Reversal (within 0.2%).
  • Step 2 (Sweep): Price sweeps below S3 ($ ext{Low} < S3 imes 0.999$), triggering retail sell stop orders.
  • Step 3 (Reclaim): Candle closes back above S3 ($ ext{Close} > S3$).
  • Entry: Buy immediately on the close of the reclaim candle.
  • Stop Loss: Placed at the sweep low or below S4 Breakdown line.
  • Target 1: Central Pivot (Book 50% profit immediately; move SL to breakeven).
  • Target 2: R4 Breakout Level.
STRATEGY 2: BUY REVERSAL BREAKDOWN — SHORT SETUP WIN RATE: ~68%

Core Concept: Failure of buyers to hold S3 support, resulting in structural liquidation towards S4.

  • Step 1 (Test): Price tests S3 Buy Reversal.
  • Step 2 (Breakdown): Candle confirms breakdown and closes below S3 ($ ext{Close} < S3$).
  • Entry Window (Strict): $ ext{LTP} < S3$ AND $ ext{LTP} ge S4$. Must enter between S3 and S4.
  • Chasing Prevention: If $ ext{LTP} < S4$, entry is BLOCKED (move completed; chasing avoided).
  • Stop Loss: Must be placed strictly at Level 6 ($S1 / R1$) or Central Pivot above entry.
  • Target 1: S4 Break Down Level (Book profit immediately).
  • Target 2: S5 Target Level.
STRATEGY 3: SELL REVERSAL PIVOT BREAKDOWN — SHORT SETUP WIN RATE: ~62%

Core Concept: Rejection at R3 Sell Reversal followed by breakdown below Central Pivot equilibrium.

  • Step 1: Price tests R3 Sell Reversal ($ ext{High} ge R3$).
  • Step 2: Rejection: price fails to sustain and closes back below R3.
  • Step 3: Central Pivot breakdown: price falls and breaks below Central Pivot.
  • Entry: Short immediately upon Central Pivot breakdown confirmation.
  • Stop Loss: Placed at R3 Sell Reversal.
  • Target 1: S3 Buy Reversal (Book profit).
  • Target 2: S4 Breakdown Level.

10. Derivatives & Quantitative Options Rules

10.1 The Lunchtime S4 Discount Trap (Option Buying)

  • Setup: During the lunchtime consolidation window (11:30 AM – 01:30 PM IST), institutional option writers push premiums down towards their daily S4 level.
  • Action: Place buy limit orders at the S4 level of Call options when the underlying index is holding above daily Open.
  • Stop Loss: Below S5 support.
  • Target: Daily midpoint or R3 resistance during the afternoon breakout window (after 02:15 PM IST).

10.2 The R5 Extreme Short (Option Writing)

  • Setup: OTM Call or Put premiums that spike to touch their own daily R5 resistance level represent statistical overextension ($> 3sigma$).
  • Action: Sell/Write OTM Call or Put options upon R5 touch.
  • Risk Control: Strict 50% premium stop-loss ($ ext{Entry} imes 1.5$).
  • Target: Cover position at ₹1.0 or let expire worthless at ₹0.
  • Index Direction Filter: Never write Calls if Index is above daily Open; never write Puts if Index is below daily Open or PCR velocity is negative.

10.3 Stock Options Expiry Logic

  • NSE Monthly Cycle: NSE stock options expire on the **last Tuesday of each month**.
  • Symbol Formatting: Strict YYMMDD format matching the last Tuesday date (e.g. 260728 for 28 July 2026).
  • S3/S4 Confluence: On the 30-minute chart, enter long only on **Bullish Marubozu** (70.6% win rate) or **Doji** (68.8% win rate) closes. Avoid red candle touches.

11. Volatility Breakouts & High Beta Setups

11.1 NR7 (Narrow Range 7) Strategy

An NR7 day occurs when the daily range is narrower than the preceding 6 sessions. This represents absolute volatility compression.

  • Long Trigger: Next session price breaks above NR7 High. Stop loss at NR7 Midpoint. Target: $2 imes$ to $3 imes$ risk.
  • Short Trigger: Next session price breaks below NR7 Low. Stop loss at NR7 Midpoint. Target: $2 imes$ to $3 imes$ risk.

11.2 20% Upper Circuit (UC) Potential Scanner
